Support

Goals keeps your routines and one-time projects in categories you make, and shows the one you've left longest at the top. It works fully offline: your lists stay on your phone unless you export them.

Need help?
Email support@freeboard.boats with questions, problems or ideas. If something isn't working, please include your phone model, its iOS or Android version, and the app version (shown under About).

How it works

Routine or one-time?

A routine (∞) never finishes: each time you do it, tap Done on its row and today's date is stamped. A one-time item (○) ends: tick its steps, then tap the circle (or the Done switch on its page) to complete it. Finished items move under "Done". You can switch an item between the two types on its page.

Why can't I tick an item as done?

A one-time item can only be completed once every one of its steps is ticked. The circle stays faded until then.

What do the colours mean?

"Last: …" shows when you last worked on the item. It turns orange after 7 days and red after 14 days, or when it has never been done. Items are listed with the most neglected first.

What counts as "working on" an item?

Tapping Done on a routine, ticking one of its steps, or completing it. You can also pick any date, or clear it, on the item's page.

Categories

Tap + at the end of the ribbon at the top to add one. Long-press a category to rename it, move it left or right, or delete it (deleting removes its items too, so export first if in doubt). An item can be moved to another category on its page.

Steps

On an item's page, type in "Add a step". Tap a step's text to edit it; clear it to delete it. With two or more steps, tap Edit to drag steps into a new order or delete them.

Deleting an item

Swipe it to the left in the list. On Android, tap Undo straight away if it was a mistake.

Backups and moving phones

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up?

Tap the share icon at the top left and export one category or all of them as a Markdown (.md) file, then save or send it where you like (Files, Google Drive, mail…). It is plain text you can open in any editor.

How do I restore, or move to a new phone?

On the new phone, open the same menu and choose Import .md file…. Import works between iPhone and Android in both directions. It adds categories and items that are missing and skips any item whose title already exists in that category, so it never overwrites what is already there.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes. Deleting the app deletes everything in it, and a lost or broken phone takes the data with it. Export a copy regularly. Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup, if it is on, may also include the app's data.

The import says it could not read my file

Import accepts text files up to 5 MB in the format the app exports. Other files (pictures, documents, very large files) are refused on purpose.

Does it remind me?

No. Goals sends no notifications and makes no alarms: open it when you want to see what you have been neglecting.
