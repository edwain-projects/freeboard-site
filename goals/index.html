One list, two kinds of things

Some things end. Some never do.

∞

Routines

Calling family, practising a language, cleaning the filters. A routine never finishes: each time you do it, tap Done and the date is stamped.

○

One-time projects

Renewing a passport, painting the balcony. Break it into steps, tick them off, and mark it done once every step is ticked.

What's inside

Small, quick, and out of your way

Your own categories

A ribbon across the top holds as many categories as you like — Goals, Projects, Home, Health. Long-press one to rename, move or delete it.

Last done, at a glance

Ticking a step, finishing an item or tapping Done all count as working on it. Change the date by hand whenever you need to.

Steps you can reorder

Edit a step right where it is, drag steps into a new order, delete the ones you no longer need.

Plain-text backup

Export one category or everything as a Markdown (.md) file — readable in any text editor. Import it on another phone, iPhone or Android; it adds what's missing and never overwrites what's there.

Light and dark

Follows your phone's appearance, on both platforms.

Private by design

Your lists stay on your phone

No account, no internet, no ads, no tracking

Goals never connects to the internet and contains no analytics or advertising code. Everything you enter is stored on your phone only. An exported file goes only where you send it. Because there is no server, keep an export as your backup — deleting the app deletes its data. Privacy policy
