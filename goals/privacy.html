Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Goals does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. This applies to the iPhone and the Android app.

  • Everything you enter (categories, items, steps, notes, dates) is stored locally on your phone only, in the app's own private storage.
  • The app makes no network connections and has no server. The Android app does not even request permission to use the internet.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code is included.
  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • The app asks for no other permissions either: no contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Files you export or import

If you use Export, the app writes a Markdown (.md) file and hands it to your phone's own share sheet or file picker; you choose where it goes (for example Files, AirDrop, Google Drive, Mail or Messages). If you use Import, the app reads only the file you pick. The developer has no access to these files or their contents.

Your phone's own backup

If you turn on your phone's backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), that backup may include the app's data. It is stored in your own Apple or Google account under their privacy terms; the developer has no access to it.

Links in the app

The About screen has links to this website and to the support email address. Tapping one opens your browser or mail app; from there, the website or your mail provider handles the request under its own terms. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking; it is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which see your IP address when they serve the page, under their own privacy terms.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or retain. Deleting an item, a category or the whole app removes that data from your phone. Uninstalling the app removes all of it, so export a copy first if you want to keep it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
