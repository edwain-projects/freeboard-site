Support

Days at Sea helps seafarers on rotation or contract work track their sea time, pay, and rotation balance directly on their device. It works fully offline — all your data stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

Need help?

Email support@seatimetracking.com with any questions, bug reports, or feature requests. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I back up my data?

Use "Export backup" in the app to save a JSON file you can store or transfer yourself. Use "Import backup" to restore it.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes — export a backup first if you plan to reinstall or switch devices.

Can I rely on the app's balance for pay disputes?

Treat it as a personal reference, not a substitute for your employer's own records — see the EULA for details.
