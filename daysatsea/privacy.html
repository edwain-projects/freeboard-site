Days at Sea Privacy Support EULA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Days at Sea does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • All information you enter (contract details, rotation schedule, sea time logs, pay data) is stored locally on your device only, using your browser/app's local storage.
  • The app makes no network connections and has no backend server.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.

If you use the app's "Export backup" feature, the resulting file is created and shared entirely by you (for example via AirDrop, Files, Mail, or Messages). The developer has no access to that file or its contents.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, share with third parties, or retain — uninstalling the app removes all local data.
