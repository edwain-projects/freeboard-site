Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Days in Dock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The developer has no server and never receives anything you put in the app.

What the app stores

  • Your projects, jobs, dates, notes, people and companies, and the photos and files you attach are saved inside the app on your phone.
  • Dated backups of each project are kept next to them, also on your phone.

What leaves the phone

Only what you choose to send: a PDF report or a project export through the Share menu, or a backup you share. Those files are created and sent by you (for example by Mail, AirDrop, WhatsApp, Files or Google Drive) and are handled by the service you pick. The developer has no access to them.

If you turn on your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), that backup may include the app's data. It is stored in your own Apple or Google account under their privacy terms; the developer has no access to it.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No location, contacts or usage data are collected.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own. The only exception is the App Store or Google Play, when you start the free trial, buy the full version or restore a purchase; that exchange is between you and Apple or Google.

Camera, photos, files and Face ID

The camera, your photo library and the file picker are used only when you attach something to a note. Face ID or your fingerprint is checked by the phone itself; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

Deleting your data

Delete a project from the Projects screen, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on the phone. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for the developer to delete, sell, or share.

Children

Days in Dock is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.
