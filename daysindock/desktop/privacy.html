Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Days in Dock Desktop Version does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The developer has no server and never receives anything you put in the app.

What the app stores

  • Your projects, jobs, dates, notes and their history, categories, statuses and phases, and the people and companies you enter (with any contact details you add), saved in a database in the app's folder.
  • The names of the app's users, with passwords stored only as one-way hashes.
  • The photos, documents, e-mails and links you attach, in the app's documents folder.
  • Backups of all of the above, in the app's backups folder, and log files of what the app did, in data/logs.

Everything stays on the computer that holds the folder.

What leaves the computer

Only what you choose to take out: PDF reports and exports, Excel exports, a full backup, or files you open in another program or copy elsewhere. The developer has no access to any of it.

Phone access is off until an admin turns it on. Then the app also answers phones on your own local network, over an encrypted connection, and only phones you paired with a QR code. A paired phone can see jobs and notes and add notes and photos. Nothing goes through the internet.

Network connections

  • The app makes no internet connections and includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or AI.
  • The app's window is served from your own computer only, and protected so that other programs can't use it.

Google Chrome

The app shows its window in Google Chrome, with a separate Chrome profile kept in the app's folder. The app starts that window with Chrome's sync, background networking, component updates, crash reporting and on-device AI model downloads turned off. Chrome is Google's product: it still follows its own settings and any policies set by your organisation, under Google's terms.

Other people's details

If you enter details of other people — contractors, shipyard contacts, colleagues — you decide what is kept and why, as you would in any record you keep yourself. You can edit those details at any time. Deleted jobs and notes stay in the job history and in the backups until those backups are replaced, so plan what you enter accordingly.

Deleting your data

Quit the app and delete its folder (including any backup copies you made elsewhere) to remove everything it stored. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for the developer to delete, sell, or share.

Children

Days in Dock Desktop Version is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.
