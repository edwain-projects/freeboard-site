Yard Privacy Support EULA

Support

Yard is a job diary and Gantt chart for refits and shipyard periods. It works fully offline — everything stays on your phone unless you share or export it.

Need help?

Email support@freeboard.boats with questions, bug reports or ideas. If you're reporting a problem, please include your phone model, its iOS or Android version, and the app version (More → About, privacy and support).

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There is no account and no server.

How do backups work, and how do I go back to an earlier version?

A backup is made when you open a project, when you leave the app and once a day, whenever something changed. The app keeps every backup of the last 7 days, then one per week for 4 weeks and one per month for 4 months. Open More → Backups and restore any of them; the current state is saved first, so you can undo a restore.

How do I keep a copy off the phone?

In More → Backups, use Share on a backup to save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or anywhere else. Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup may also include the app's data.

How do I move a project to a new phone, or give it to someone?

In Projects, choose Export on the project and send the file. On the other phone, open Projects and tap Import. It works between iPhone and Android.

How do I attach a document from my computer?

Put it in a cloud drive (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox), then in the app add a note to the job and pick the file. On Android you can also share files into Yard from other apps, such as your mail.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is deleted or hidden. You can still open your projects, make reports, export and back up. Adding and changing things needs the one-time unlock (US$9.99).

I bought the full version on another phone, or reinstalled the app.

Open More → Full version and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes — the app's data and its backups are removed with it. Export your projects or share a backup first.
